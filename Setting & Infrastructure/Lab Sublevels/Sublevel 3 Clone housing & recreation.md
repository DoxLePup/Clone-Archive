Sublevel 3 is where clones **live, rest, and simply exist**. It’s the most personal and human part of the lab, designed with comfort and self-expression in mind. While the deeper levels are built for purpose, this one is built for _presence_. It isn’t a facility—it’s home.

Each clone is assigned a **private living space**, tailored to their preferences. Rooms are modular and adjustable, allowing for personalized layouts, décor, and environmental controls. Some clones choose minimal, utilitarian setups. Others customize every corner with personal art, lighting, plants, or sensory tools that help them relax or focus. The lab encourages this personalization—it reinforces the idea that they are not tools, but people.

That said, **not all clones live here full-time**. Some choose to spend most of their time outside the lab—traveling, working, or simply living their own lives. These clones often still maintain their quarters within Sublevel 3, returning periodically for rest, reflection, or to retrieve personal items. Their rooms may become shrines to past selves, quiet places where they reconnect to the network in solitude. There's no requirement to stay in the lab—just the freedom to return whenever needed.

Common areas include:

- A **communal lounge**, complete with cozy furniture, media access, and memory-projection screens that allow shared recall of archived events

- A **kitchen and dining space**, used more for bonding than nourishment, where clones can prepare meals, replicate snacks, or just talk

- A **fitness and physical maintenance room**, designed to accommodate a wide range of physical presentations and combat training styles


While clones don’t physically tire the same way non-clones do, the space still encourages relaxation, decompression, and personal rituals. Some clones spend long hours here alone, processing memories in silence. Others gather, play games, share jokes, or debate philosophy. Bonds form naturally in these shared spaces, not because SPINE suggests it, but because **they want to**.

The atmosphere is warm, dimmable, and intentionally unstructured. It’s the only level where the walls have art painted directly onto them—some abstract, some deeply personal. The halls are quieter here. You’ll hear laughter one moment and complete stillness the next. There’s no schedule, no orders, no structure beyond mutual respect.

If SPINE is the system that connects them, Sublevel 3 is where they learn what that connection means. Not just as a network—but as a family.
