Sublevel 7 is the **deepest and most restricted** area of the lab—a place of intense focus, controlled risk, and quiet boundaries. It is divided between high-security containment and advanced experimental research, with only select personnel and authorized clones granted access. While the upper levels are designed with warmth and identity in mind, this level is clinical, sterile, and serious—built for understanding the unknown, not living in it.

This floor includes several distinct divisions:

---

#### **Calypsi Research Wing**

This section is entirely dedicated to the ongoing investigation of **Calypsi’s curse**. The curse, which ties her to a slow, painful transformation into a full dragon, is not well understood—even by Calypsi herself. The lab’s agreement with her to donate her DNA in exchange for research assistance resulted in this wing’s creation.

Here, researchers examine:

- Magical residue left in her genetic material

- Biological effects of her transformation triggers

- Psychological data from transformation episodes

- Potential mitigation protocols or divine resonance analysis


This wing is part biological, part metaphysical. Some of the research borders on the arcane, combining science with speculative theology. A few clones who are especially curious—or emotionally invested—sometimes assist with studies here. Others avoid it, aware of the emotional weight the space carries. The room holds a quiet reverence for the sacrifice Calypsi made, and the question that hangs in the air: _Can this be undone?_

---

#### **Synthetic Development Lab**

A newer section of Sublevel 7, this lab is focused on **Android and synthetic lifeform creation**. Separate from the clone program, this initiative explores the development of artificial beings not born from organic DNA but crafted from advanced circuitry, synthetic tissue, and artificial intelligence.

Projects include:

- Android body frame design and synthetic muscle systems

- Neural interface architecture and consciousness simulation

- Emotion emulation and artificial social bonding studies

- Hybrid biological-synthetic prototypes


These experiments are in early stages, but their presence represents the lab’s long-term vision: to understand not just memory and identity, but consciousness in all its forms. Some of the synthetic prototypes show promise. Others raise questions the team isn’t ready to answer.

---

#### **General Research & Hazard Containment**

This includes standard experimentation in gene manipulation, memory augmentation, and SPINE-adjacent development. Alongside these efforts is a controlled research program involving willing test subjects, who voluntarily participate in approved experimental trials. These studies focus on power interaction, physiological limits, cognitive resilience, and long-term adaptation under carefully monitored conditions. All participation is consent-based, documented, and subject to strict ethical oversight, with immediate termination protocols if instability or harm is detected.

Beside this research area sits the high-security containment wing, where unstable or dangerous elements are isolated. These include corrupted clone husks, failed integrations, hostile data clusters, or any external entities deemed unsafe for the broader lab. Each containment unit is sealed and continuously monitored, with harsh lockdown protocols in place. This part of Sublevel 7 is not visited lightly. If something is stored here, it means it’s not ready, or should never be released.

---

Sublevel 7 exists as a boundary between the lab’s highest hopes and its deepest caution. Every breakthrough here could change the world—or tear it apart. That’s why only the most trusted are allowed through its doors. Not because of danger alone… but because **understanding always comes with a cost.**
