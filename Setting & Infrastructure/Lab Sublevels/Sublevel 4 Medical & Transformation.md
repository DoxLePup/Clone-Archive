Sublevel 4 is a space of **care, recovery, and transition**. It serves two primary functions: maintaining the physical and mental health of clones, and safely facilitating their divine transformation between male and female forms. This level is quiet, clean, and deeply respectful—designed not just for clinical efficiency, but for emotional safety and trust.

The **medical wing** includes:

- Comprehensive health monitoring systems tailored to clone physiology

- Non-invasive diagnostics, regeneration chambers, and restorative treatments

- Private recovery rooms for both short-term rest and deeper psychological care

- Counseling spaces for emotional processing, equipped for both clone and non-clone therapists


Though clones are biologically resilient, they are not immune to injury or emotional strain. Accidents, stress, death of another clone, or memory overload can leave an imprint. This wing ensures that **no pain goes unattended**, and that clones have the time and space to heal on their own terms.

Adjacent to the medical sector is the **transformation chamber**—a softly lit, climate-controlled room designed to allow clones to shift forms in total comfort and privacy. While their divine transformation is seamless and under their control, the physical, emotional, and social implications can still be intense. The room supports them through:

- Ambient temperature controls and sensory modulation

- Adjustable lighting and mirrors for visual feedback

- Clean clothing, body-safe hygiene products, and support tools for post-shift comfort

- Emergency alert systems in case a clone is overwhelmed or disoriented


Despite the ease of the transformation itself, some clones find comfort in the ritual of the space—the familiarity, the quiet, the sense of being alone but safe. Others don’t use the room at all, preferring to transform in private quarters or while out in the world. Its use is optional, but its presence is reassuring.

The emotional tone of Sublevel 4 is **gentle and humanizing**. It’s the only place in the lab where the lights dim automatically if a clone remains still too long. Where chairs are designed for curling into, and sounds are softened. No one rushes here. No one watches. Everything is designed to say: _You are safe. You are allowed to change._

Whether recovering, reflecting, or transitioning, Sublevel 4 gives clones something the rest of the lab can’t—**permission to be vulnerable**.
