Sublevel 6 is a space for **grief, reflection, and remembrance.** Unlike the clinical feel of the creation levels or the analytical hum of SPINE, this level is quiet—almost sacred. The air feels still. The lights are low. It’s not just a record hall—it’s a place where clones can honor the lives that came before them, and process the weight of what was lost.

This level serves two central purposes:

1. **The Physical Memorial Room** – A softly lit chamber filled with curated personal artifacts from fallen clones. Items are not displayed in a sterile museum style—they are placed with intention. A drawing. A weapon. A cup. A piece of clothing. Each object tells a story that still lives within SPINE but is no longer active in the physical world. The room is quiet, warm, and minimally observed. Clones may sit for hours here in silence, speak to the objects, or leave their own tributes.
   There are no rules in this space. Only memory.

2. **The Digital Memory Archive** – A terminal chamber where clones can access full memory profiles of those who have passed. These are not just cold data logs; they are rich, immersive reflections of who a clone was—their thoughts, experiences, emotions, and even last words. The system allows playback through written transcription, visual simulation, or full immersive stream for those who want to revisit moments in detail. Some clones come here to learn. Others to say goodbye. And some, just to remember.


This level also includes **private alcoves** for emotional processing, including quiet soundproofed spaces, writing pads, and audio diaries. Clones who struggle with grief—or simply need to feel something privately—often retreat here without needing to explain.

Memorials are held here when a clone dies. They’re not formal, but they’re powerful. Sometimes it’s just a few close friends. Other times, the entire clone network makes time to show up. SPINE keeps track of who visits and leaves notes or memories behind, archiving even the acts of remembrance themselves.

It’s also tradition to visit this level when a **new clone is born**, particularly when their induction follows a loss. Clones will often stop by this level before welcoming a new sibling—not as a requirement, but as a quiet nod to what came before.

If Sublevel 3 is home, and Sublevel 2 is the heart, Sublevel 6 is the **soul** of the lab. A place to cry, remember, and most importantly—to keep living.
