# Laboratory Report – Initial Controlled Test:
Subject Gabs
File ID: AUREL-GBS-001
Date: [Redacted]
Location: [[Sublevel 7 Research & Containment]]
Observers: Dr. J. K. Boivin, MawLex, Research Assistants [Redacted]
##### Subject Overview
Designation: Gabs
Species: Caticorn (Unicorn/Cat Hybrid)
Height / Weight: 5’2” | 130 lbs
Known Ability: Radiant Bloom ( [[Goddess Astraea]]-derived gift)
##### Purpose of Test
Establish baseline functionality and environmental impact of subject’s power in a controlled chamber setting.
##### Procedure
The subject was instructed to enter a neutral environment with full biometric monitoring (respiration, heart rate, aura signature). Emotional stimuli were presented in escalating order:
- Neutral baseline (breathing exercises, neutral lighting)
- Joy (music, positive imagery)
- Sorrow (guided memory prompts)
- Anger (stress induction via recall exercise)

MawLex remained in-chamber to observe direct effects and provide stabilization if required.

#### Results
###### Baseline:
No uncontrolled manifestation. Subject’s aura presents as faint but detectable warmth.
###### Joy Stimulus:
Horn activated spontaneously, producing motes of golden light resembling petals.
Ambient chamber temperature increased by ~2°C.
Observers reported elevated mood, lowered anxiety markers.
Effect classified as low-level restorative aura.
###### Sorrow Stimulus:
Subject’s aura condensed into a semi-visible cocoon of violet-tinted petals.
Energy field exhibited shielding qualities, reducing external magical interference by ~34%.
Subject displayed emotional strain but remained functional.
###### Anger Stimulus:
Subject produced a sudden high-intensity flash, duration ~3.2 seconds.
Resulted in temporary disorientation of observers and MawLex.
No physical harm detected; however, intensity is considered potentially weaponized under duress.
Conclusions

Radiant Bloom is directly tied to emotional state, not deliberate casting.
Effects are area-of-effect in nature, not single-target.
Residual aura persists for several minutes post-manifestation.
Subject demonstrates no intent to harm, but lack of emotional regulation presents unpredictability risk in unstable environments.
Recommendations
Initiate controlled emotional regulation training with MawLex as secondary observer.
Monitor long-term effects of aura exposure on SPINE-connected individuals.
Restrict subject’s involvement in field operations until stability threshold is confirmed.
Grant subject provisional clearance as Guest Affiliate – Non-Containment.

Signed:
Dr. J. K. Boivin
Lead Director, Sublevel 7 Research
