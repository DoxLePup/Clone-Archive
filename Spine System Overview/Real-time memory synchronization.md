SPINE’s core function is the constant, real-time synchronization of memory and cognitive data between all active clones. When one clone learns something—whether it’s a new skill, key information, or a lived experience—that data is instantly uploaded to the network and becomes available to the rest. This keeps all clones aligned, aware, and highly coordinated.

Despite this constant flow of information, clones remain individuals. Their emotional reactions, interpretations, and behavioral choices are still unique to them. While all clones may recall the same event, they can feel and react to it differently depending on their personality and context. This autonomy is preserved even within the shared system.

Each clone is connected to SPINE through their own “stem”—a personal data link that branches into the larger network. Within each stem is a conscious, accessible “private folder,” a mental partition where a clone can choose to temporarily withhold or delay information from reaching the broader network. Think of it like an internal staging area. If a clone wishes to keep a memory, feeling, or experience to themselves—whether briefly or permanently—they can consciously direct that data to stay private.

Meanwhile, the central SPINE system contains the “public folder”—a shared repository that holds everything clones make available for all to access. This public data is updated in real time, with changes to memory, knowledge, or interpretation syncing across all stems. A clone can choose at any moment to move something from private to public, or vice versa, depending on how they feel, who’s present, or what’s happening.

This architecture allows clones to protect their boundaries without disrupting network integrity. It’s especially important during emotionally intense experiences or private moments, where immediate sharing could be overwhelming, inappropriate, or unsafe.

The result is a balance between total awareness and personal space—clones are never forced to share everything, but they always have the option to, with full control over how and when.
