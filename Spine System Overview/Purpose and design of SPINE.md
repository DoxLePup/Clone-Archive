SPINE (Shared Processing and Intelligence Network Engine) was designed to overcome the limitations of its predecessor, CORE. Its purpose is to link fully developed clones into a unified yet flexible memory network, enabling constant, real-time exchange of cognitive information between units. SPINE is not involved in the creation of clones—it only connects to them once they are fully formed and ready.

SPINE functions as both a live processing system and a permanent memory archive. It doesn’t control the clones or enforce synchronization; instead, it allows them to freely access and contribute to the network while retaining full autonomy. Think of it as a shared mental operating environment where every clone has access to every other’s knowledge, experiences, and decisions.

## The design emphasizes:

#### - Instant knowledge distribution without forcing behavioral conformity
#### - Individual agency within a shared cognitive space
#### - Durable memory retention, ensuring nothing is lost—even when a clone dies
#### - Emotional insulation, with no automatic transmission of physical sensations or emotional states unless specifically accessed

SPINE ensures that no knowledge, skill, or experience is ever truly lost. Even if a clone dies, their data remains preserved in the network for all others to recall.
