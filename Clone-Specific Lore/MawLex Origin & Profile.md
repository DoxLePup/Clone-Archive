MawLex represents the stabilized genetic structure from which all SPINE-connected clones are created. While no singular clone is the original or prototype, MawLex serves as the standard template—built from the fusion of DoxLe’s damaged DNA and the restorative contribution of Calypsi, a wolf-dragon hybrid who offered her genetics in exchange for the lab's help in researching her curse.

This genetic merging resulted in a resilient, magically adaptable body compatible with SPINE. All current clones share this physical form, differing only when expressing themselves in male or female presentation. While memory and experience diverge, their bodies remain identical in structure and potential.

Physically, MawLex clones stand at 6'2" with a 6-foot tail that fades in color toward the tip, mirroring their hair. The body is slightly thick—curvy and soft when feminine, broad and solid when masculine. Their skin is human-pale beneath a layer of fur that transitions from green to light green, fading into blue near the extremities. Blue scales appear along the back, shoulders, outer thighs and hips, with smaller patches on the hands, feet, and beneath the eyes. Their eyes are bright pink, as are the paw pads. Their hair is long, starting light blue and fading into soft pink. Horns—dark, nearly black—curl back from their head. The left ear is folded, the right stands upright. A pink flower tattoo with a black thorny stem marks the right thigh, and the tail’s fur fades in the same soft blue-to-pink pattern as the hair.

Each clone has full control over gender expression, physical features, and reproductive traits, granted through divine magic by the goddess of their universe [[Goddess Astraea]]. Preferences vary between clones; some favor a consistent presentation, while others shift depending on emotional state or what they believe would best comfort or connect with someone else.

Emotionally, MawLex is introspective and multifaceted. They are thoughtful and often deeply affected by memory and context. Many clones with this build choose to dwell more on emotional nuances within the SPINE system, even though they are not required to. While they do not feel others’ emotions through SPINE, they often reflect more meaningfully on shared memories and exhibit a strong sense of empathy.

Though not leaders by hierarchy, MawLex clones are often viewed as grounding figures. They may suggest names for new clones, initiate bonding traditions, or act as emotional support for others in the network. This behavior isn’t universal but is frequently seen across multiple iterations.
