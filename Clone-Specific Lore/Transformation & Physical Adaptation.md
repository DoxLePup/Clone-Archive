SPINE-based clones possess a unique divine ability to transform between two distinct physical forms—**one male and one female**. This transformation is not the result of science or genetics but a sacred gift granted by the goddess of their universe [[Goddess Astraea]]. It is seamless, painless, and under the full conscious control of the clone. The transformation includes complete physiological change between the two forms, each stable and distinct.

Although they cannot freely customize or reshape their bodies beyond these two forms, **each form allows for limited subcategorization**. Clones can, for example, choose to maintain male reproductive organs while retaining female-presenting breasts, or reverse that pairing, depending on their personal identity, preference, or relationship context. These combinations are deliberate and meaningful choices, not cosmetic flourishes.

What’s important is that the clones **do not have a “default” body.** All SPINE-connected clones share the same base template and have access to the same male and female forms. No one form is considered more “real” or “original” than another. Every clone decides for themselves how they present, and that presentation may change over time or remain consistent for years.

Many clones shift between forms as needed, while others remain in one form long-term. Some choose a form based on how they feel inside; others may shift depending on what feels appropriate in their environment or relationships. A few clones, especially those with strong emotional bonds to others, may even adapt their form based on what they think will make someone else more comfortable—a gesture of care rather than obligation.

The divine origin of this transformation means it cannot be imitated, removed, or altered by scientific means. It is a core part of their being—something sacred and enduring, tied directly to their spiritual makeup rather than their biology. Despite being gifted with such transformative flexibility, clones do not treat it as a novelty. For many, it's a powerful and deeply personal expression of identity.

In daily life, this transformation does not disrupt their mental clarity, SPINE connection, or emotional state. It is as natural as breathing—part of who they are, not something they do. The existence of two consistent, stable forms grants clones freedom through structure: the freedom to express, adapt, and relate, all while staying true to their embodied self.
