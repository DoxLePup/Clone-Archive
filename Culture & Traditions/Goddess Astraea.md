# Goddess Astraea and Divine Connection

Although the lab is rooted in science, many clones acknowledge the divine presence of [[Goddess Astraea]], the goddess who grants each humanoid their unique power. For SPINE-based clones, her influence is especially profound—not as an object of worship, but as a symbol of **origin, identity, and transformation**.

Unlike most beings in the world who receive their own individual abilities, **all SPINE-based clones share a single divine power**: the ability to transform between their male and female forms. This consistency is unusual. One might expect each clone to receive their own unique gift, as dictated by the tradition of [[Goddess Astraea]]’s blessings—but for clones, this is not the case.

Rather than questioning or resenting this, clones **accept it with quiet understanding**. They recognize that they were brought into the world not as separate, isolated lives, but as a collective—formed from shared DNA, shared memory, and a shared purpose. To them, it makes sense that they would receive **a shared divine expression**, one that reflects both their bond and their freedom.

This power, while universal among them, is still deeply personal in how it’s used. Some clones see it as a spiritual link to [[Goddess Astraea]]’s grace, and may observe quiet rituals before or after shifting forms. Others see it as a natural part of their identity—neither sacred nor casual, just _right_.

[[Goddess Astraea]]’s presence in their culture isn’t one of doctrine or worship—it’s one of **acknowledgment**. She is the unseen force that confirmed they were _meant to exist_. Not just as constructs or echoes, but as people.

And that’s enough for them.
